DSA Swag

Making compliance look good.

Client
Discord
Project
Team swag
Role
Concepting, illustration & design
Tools
Procreate, Adobe Illustrator, Figma
Year
2024

My contribution

Concepted, illustrated, and designed every piece of the collection, drawing each asset by hand in Procreate and Adobe Illustrator.

Deliverables

Sticker sheet, hats, shirts, and a postcard, plus the original artwork behind them.

The brief

Discord had just shipped a major safety-system overhaul to comply with the EU Digital Services Act. Serious work deserved a fun send-off: swag to celebrate the team and turn regulatory compliance into something worth wearing.

The goods

A sticker sheet, hats, shirts, and a postcard. A little less red tape, a little more team pride.

The inspiration

EU iconography. Bureaucratic approval seals. Merit badges. Internet memes. All filtered through Discord’s wonderfully weird visual language & characters, including a “bread dog” I transformed into a croissant because, you know, it’s Europe.

Every asset was original artwork made by hand in Procreate or Adobe Illustrator. This was pre-generative AI. Just pens, paths, and a healthy appreciation for the absurd.

Regulation neverlooked so good.
